# Photography

The landing page carries no photography — it's typographic, and the yellow
ACSM band does the work the hero images used to. One slot remains:

| File | Where it appears | Crop | Minimum size | What the shot needs to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `auth-panel.jpg` | Sign-in and sign-up, right panel | Portrait, tall | 1200 × 1600 | Fills half a desktop screen. Calm and atmospheric — a caption sits over the bottom third, so keep that area uncluttered and darker. |

Drop a file with that exact name here and it appears automatically — no code
change. Until then the slot renders drawn artwork (`PosterArt`), so the page
looks deliberate rather than broken either way.

`hero-lifting.jpg` and `hero-coach.jpg` are no longer referenced by anything.
They still ship in every build, so delete them unless photography is coming
back to the landing page.

## If you add photography later

The audience is affluent and will recognise stock photography instantly.
Prefer real photographs of your own coaches and your own space, even if
they're less polished — a genuine photo of a real gym outperforms a perfect
stock image of somebody else's.

Export at roughly twice the size above, then compress — aim under 300 KB.
Images are bundled into the static site, so every megabyte is something a
member downloads on mobile data before they see anything.
