## Choosing them

The audience is affluent and will recognise stock photography instantly.
Prefer real photographs of your own coaches and your own space, even if they're
less polished — a genuine photo of a real gym outperforms a perfect stock image
of somebody else's.

If you must use stock, avoid: white-background studio shots, visible watermarks,
exaggerated expressions, and anything with a clearly American commercial-gym
look. Warm, desaturated, natural light sits best against this palette.

## Preparing them

Export at roughly twice the minimum size above, then compress — aim under
300 KB each. They're bundled into the static site, so every megabyte is
something a member downloads on mobile data before they see anything.
